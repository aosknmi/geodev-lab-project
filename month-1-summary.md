# Month 1 Summary

## Question

Which areas in Oyo State fall outside a 5 km buffer zone around health facilities?

## Operation Used

I used the **Buffer** operation to create a 5 km buffer zone around health facilities in Oyo State. I chose the Buffer operation because it directly addresses part of the question by showing the areas within 5 km of health facilities and, consequently, the areas that fall outside the coverage zone.

## What I Expected and What I Got

I expected the 5 km buffer analysis to show the areas of Oyo State that are within and outside the 5 km coverage zone of health facilities.

The result matched my expectation. The analysis clearly showed the areas that fall outside the 5 km buffer zone around the health facilities.

## What Surprised Me

The analysis showed that some areas of Oyo State fall outside the 5 km coverage zone of the health facilities. This indicates that there are areas that may be relatively far from the mapped health facilities.

## What Data I Still Need

To better understand the areas outside the 5 km coverage zone, I would need additional data such as:

- Population data for the affected areas.
- Locations of settlements and communities.
- Health facility capacity and available services.
- More complete and up-to-date health facility data.

These additional datasets would help provide more context about the population affected and accessibility to health facilities in the areas outside the 5 km buffer zone.
